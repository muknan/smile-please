# Smile Please

Smile Please connects people in Delhi with free dental care and oral health awareness.

## Language

**Care request**: A patient's request for help finding a dentist, before a time has been agreed.

**Appointment**: A care record that moves from request or booking through confirmation, completion, or cancellation. Rescheduling changes that same appointment.

**Slot**: A dentist's offered time and capacity for appointments.

**Hold**: A short reservation that gives one booking session time to finish its form. It is not a confirmed appointment.

**Patient**: The person receiving care. Knowing their email address does not prove authority to change their account.

**Dentist**: A volunteer professional whose registration must be checked before activation.

**Partnership inquiry**: An organisation's proposal to collaborate, awaiting a feasibility discussion. It is not a donation or a promise of a programme.

**Consent**: Permission for a specific use of personal information, independently withdrawable from other purposes.
